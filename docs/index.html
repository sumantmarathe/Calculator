Privacy Policy for PrismCalc Calculator

Effective Date: October 28, 2026
Application ID: com.prismcalc.calculator

1. Overview & Zero-Data-Collection Guarantee

PrismCalc Calculator ("the Application") is engineered as a strictly offline utility. We do not collect, transmit, store on external servers, share, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, usage telemetry, or calculation history.

2. Permissions Requested

  • No Internet Access: The Application does not request android.permission.INTERNET or android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE. It is incapable of transmitting data off your device.
  • No Storage, Contacts, Location, or Identity Access: No access to files, location, camera, microphone, contacts, or accounts is ever requested.
  • Tactile Vibration Only (android.permission.VIBRATE): Used strictly for optional keypad haptic feedback.

3. Local On-Device Storage

All user preferences (skin selection, active calculator mode, angle unit, word size, haptics/audio toggles) and calculation history entries are stored strictly on-device inside the Application's private sandbox. Cloud backup is disabled (android:allowBackup="false"). You can clear your history at any time from the in-app History Tape.

4. Third-Party SDKs & Analytics

The Application contains zero third-party analytics, crash-reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs, and requires no sign-in or login account.
