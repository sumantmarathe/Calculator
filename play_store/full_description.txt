PrismCalc: Multi-Mode Calculator (Normal, Scientific & Programmer)

Experience precision engineering and tactile design on Android. PrismCalc combines three specialized calculation engines with distinct, customizable visual skins—built from the ground up with 100% offline execution, zero permissions, and zero data collection.

THREE POWERFUL CALCULATION MODES
1. Normal Mode:
• High-precision 128-bit decimal arithmetic (34 significant figures) eliminating floating-point rounding artifacts (0.1 + 0.2 = 0.3 exact).
• Full operator precedence (PEMDAS/BODMAS) with smart parentheses and live expression preview as you type.
• Contextual percentage calculations (markup, discount, ratios) and dedicated Memory keys (MC, MR, M-, M+) with live memory indicator.

2. Scientific Mode:
• Complete trigonometric suite (sin, cos, tan, asin, acos, atan) across Degrees (DEG), Radians (RAD), and Gradians (GRAD) with exact unit-circle boundary lookup tables.
• Hyperbolic and inverse hyperbolic functions (sinh, cosh, tanh, asinh, acosh, atanh).
• Logarithmic & exponential operations: natural log (ln), base-10 log (log10), base-2 log (log2), e^x, 10^x, arbitrary powers (x^y), square root, cube root, reciprocal (1/x), and absolute value.
• Exact large-integer factorials (up to 1000!) and high-precision mathematical constants (π and e).

3. Programmer Mode:
• Live simultaneous 4-base synchronization across Hexadecimal (HEX), Decimal (DEC), Octal (OCT), and Binary (BIN).
• Selectable hardware word sizes: QWORD (64-bit), DWORD (32-bit), WORD (16-bit), and BYTE (8-bit) with two's complement Signed and Unsigned modes.
• Full bitwise logic suite: AND, OR, XOR, NOT, NAND, NOR, plus bit shifts (LSH, RSH) and bit rotations (ROL, ROR).
• Interactive 64-bit binary toggle matrix allowing direct bit inspection and single-tap bit flipping.

DISTINCT CUSTOMIZABLE VISUAL SKINS
Transform the entire look, typography, geometry, and tactile feel of your calculator:
• Retro Pixel B/W Skin: Crisp 1-bit monochrome dot-matrix LCD aesthetic with monospace pixel typography, sharp 0dp corners, and heavy tactile borders.
• Modern Minimalist Skin: Sleek contemporary dark slate aesthetic with squircle geometry, subtle elevation, and refined typography.
• Futuristic Cyber HUD Skin: Sci-fi HUD interface with neon cyan, magenta, and amber accents, chamfered cut-corner geometry, and subtle scanline overlays.

TACTILE HAPTICS, PROCEDURAL AUDIO & RESPONSIVE LAYOUTS
• Skin-Aware Haptic Feedback: Custom vibration profiles tailored to each skin (crisp mechanical tick for Retro Pixel, subtle micro-pulse for Modern, double-pulse HUD feedback for Futuristic).
• Zero-Asset Procedural Audio Synthesizer: Optional real-time synthesized 16-bit PCM button click waveforms generated in memory.
• Persistent Local History Tape: Review past calculations, tap any entry to recall its result, or tap EXPR to re-edit the full expression.
• Adaptive Layouts: Automatically adapts to Phone Portrait, Phone Landscape (split display & keypad), and Tablet/Foldable Dual-Pane Workstation layouts.

STRICT PRIVACY & ZERO TELEMETRY
• 100% Offline: Requires zero internet, network, storage, contacts, or location permissions.
• No Sign-In or Accounts: Open the app and calculate immediately.
• Zero Tracking: No analytics SDKs, no crash-reporting telemetry, no ads, and cloud backup explicitly disabled (`android:allowBackup="false"`) so your calculation history never leaves your device.
