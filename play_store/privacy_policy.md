# Privacy Policy for PrismCalc Calculator

**Effective Date:** October 28, 2026  
**Application ID:** `com.prismcalc.calculator`

## 1. Overview & Zero-Data-Collection Guarantee
PrismCalc Calculator ("the Application") is designed and engineered as a strictly offline utility. We do **not** collect, transmit, store on external servers, share, or sell any personal data, device identifiers, usage telemetry, or calculation history.

## 2. Permissions Requested
The Application requests **zero** sensitive or network permissions:
- **No Internet Access:** The Application does not declare `android.permission.INTERNET` or `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`. It is technically incapable of establishing network connections or transmitting data off your device.
- **No Storage, Contacts, Location, or Identity Access:** The Application requests no access to your photos, files, location, microphone, camera, contacts, or device accounts.
- **Tactile Vibration Only (`android.permission.VIBRATE`):** Used solely to provide optional tactile haptic feedback when pressing calculator keys. This can be toggled off at any time in the in-app settings.

## 3. Local On-Device Storage
All user preferences (selected visual skin, active calculator mode, angle unit, word size, haptics/audio toggles) and calculation history entries are stored strictly locally inside the Application's private internal sandbox directory on your device.
- Android Auto-Backup is explicitly disabled (`android:allowBackup="false"`) to guarantee that your local calculation history is never uploaded to cloud backup servers.
- You can clear your calculation history at any time using the **CLEAR** button in the History Tape, or by clearing app storage / uninstalling the Application.

## 4. Third-Party SDKs & Analytics
The Application contains **zero** third-party analytics SDKs, crash reporting tools, advertising networks, or tracking libraries. No sign-in or user account is ever required.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Because the Application collects zero personal or device information from any user, it is safe for users of all ages and fully complies with COPPA and GDPR requirements.

## 6. Changes to This Policy
Any future updates to this Privacy Policy will be included inside the Application repository and published alongside Google Play Store updates.

## 7. Contact
If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy or the offline architecture of PrismCalc Calculator, please contact the developer via the support email listed on the Google Play Store listing page.
